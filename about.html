Our story

Rooted in Fourways. Focused on your future.

WealthPro Financial Consultants is a dynamic small and medium-sized accounting and consulting firm based in Fourways, Johannesburg.

We provide trusted corporate compliance, finance and consulting services to established and emerging businesses, NPOs, co-operatives and similar organisations.

Our work is grounded in a simple belief: when business owners understand their numbers and meet their obligations, they gain the freedom to make better decisions.

That is why we pair accurate technical work with clear guidance, responsive service and a genuine commitment to transferring knowledge.

Our mission

Sound advice. Trusted delivery.

To provide sound financial, compliance and consultation services while upholding honesty, integrity, confidentiality, professional competence and due care for every client and stakeholder.

Our vision

Growth that creates opportunity.

Our five-year medium-term plan focuses on growth, skills transfer and evolving WealthPro from an SMME into a well-established role player across Gauteng and the rest of South Africa.

We believe practical knowledge shared with our team and stakeholders is one of the strongest safeguards against the non-compliance that holds many SMMEs back.

How we work

Professional standards. Personal attention.

We combine technical precision with a relationship-led approach, keeping your organisation informed and prepared.

  • We explain obligations in practical, accessible language
  • We build solutions around your organisation’s stage and goals
  • We protect confidentiality and act with professional care
  • We transfer knowledge that strengthens your internal capability
